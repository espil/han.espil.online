Left - Travel, Freedom

The game is a journey

Left is freedom: of the four directions, this is the one that leaves the house. It holds the journey - the game that is travel, played on your own two feet.

People ask what the game in AIO is. It is travel. Espil Online is the world I am building inside the Cauldron, and you do not watch it from above: you enter a real city and walk it yourself, at street level, on your own two feet.

I studied tourism because I hold a conviction about it. A screen can introduce a culture, and social channels do their best at sharing one, but they only attempt it. Only visiting a place in person and walking its streets yourself truly shares a culture. So the game is built as traveling: real streets, real building footprints and heights, real weather of a place - a journey first, and a game because the journey is one.

The five-direction design

AIO is divided by a simple design strategy of mine: split the sections so each one reads as a page of its own. The centre is the main page. Around it, each direction carries its own value: the left is the past, and freedom; the right is the future, and the decision; the top is dreams, and the tools for them; the bottom is the current state, where things stand now.

Each section can divide again into four directions of its own, so you reach different sections without pressing a lot of buttons. It works the way a catalogue does in an RPG: open one, and the branch of choices on that theme unfolds. I find the tactic extremely self-intuitive - you always know where you are, and where a choice leads.

Move with the edge buttons, the arrow keys, or the buttons inside each room. Every direction branches once more beyond its wing, so keep going outward. Escape returns to the gate. The demo shop inside AIO is Kuro Sushi; its hours are Tuesday to Sunday, 11:30 am to 10:00 pm.

The story

Espil began with an obsession: a house I could not stop designing. Minimal, 3D-printed, set down in nature without disturbing it, and powered by its own hydroelectric and solar generation rather than the grid. The Espil House Collection is that obsession made into houses - each one a complete 3D habitat, VR-ready, editable and printable, with a tree that belongs to it alone.

Inside the idea sits a VR chamber that copies a place completely - its temperature, its humidity, its wind - so that being somewhere can be shared, not just shown. The houses and their models are traded inside Espil.Online, and one day they stop being a collection and become an address: the housing layer of the world I am building, where your website is your house and these designs are its first homes.

That is why these pages exist. A collection should be somewhere its maker controls, introduced in full, with its spec sheets and its story attached - not scattered across someone else's marketplace. So the houses live here now.

Provenance: the Espil House Collection and the Espil Ceil House Addons were first minted on OpenSea in 2022. Their home is this page.
