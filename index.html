Worlds, Games & Experiments

  • Cauldron — ESPIL Online | The Cauldron 2025 — the Mapbox RPG prototype; see Selected Works.
  • Capilano Theft Auto 2024 — a GTA-inspired game concept for Capilano University, with its own CTA Bot. Its online twin, Capilano Theft Online, was my real-earth experiment: Unity + Photon multiplayer over a Google Earth map (Cesium), a character set loose on the real city — VR was the goal; practical limits said otherwise. The experiment the current Espil.Online map grew from.
  • OpenAI Presentation 2023 — “OpenAI — Decision Making”, presented as a 3D flip-book.
  • Sushi Umi — flip-book 2023 — a 3D flip-book story promo for the restaurant.
  • JEHB — a small personal progressive web app, built with Vite.

Ongijongi Family

  • Ongijongi 2023–24 — the musical organisation's promo site: events, casting (mozip) and its Saturday programme, with 3D and music.
  • Ongijongi Media 2022 — a video-sharing platform for the troupe, on CumulusClips.
  • Ongijongi Register 2022 — the Musical Society's member application page.

School Projects

  • Boundless Horizon Camps 2025 — a parallax camping site with a chatbot demo. The BHC idea underneath it: drones and VR turning a campsite forest into a game - a dream I still hold for Espil.Online.
  • Boundless Horizon Campground 2025 — a glamping site with chatbot, tied to my documented security & IP framework.
  • RAM — Random Access Memory, taken literally: a funeral and memorial company that loads an applicant's conversations and memories into an AI, so that through VR glasses you can speak with the soul again. A school concept I still dream of bringing into Espil.Online.
  • Sabai — Beats and Bridges 2024 — a multimedia project on Vancouver's EDM scene and the artist SABAI: interview, 360° and 3D.
  • Ryde — a BCIT transit project that integrated the TransLink API wholesale: real routes, real data, end to end. Its SkyTrain stop database runs live on this site.
  • Pantro — The Secret Diary of Espil Ceil — a 3D flip-book introduction to the Espil Ceil diary world, animated with GSAP.
  • coffee_spies — a BCIT app prototype.
  • Music Pond 2021 — a BCIT school project; I designed the user interface.
