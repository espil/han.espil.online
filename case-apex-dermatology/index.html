Case Study

Apex Dermatology: One Clinic, One Web System

A dermatology clinic does not need four disconnected web projects. It needs one system a patient can move through without thinking: learn, ask, book.

Client
Apex Dermatology - a dermatology clinic
Delivered
Figma design, Apex Derm landing site, Servo chatbot reservations, QDerm online booking with calendar sync and email notifications
Evidence
The delivered site lives at /apex/

The problem

The clinic's web presence had to cover everything at once: present the practice, answer patients before they call, and take real bookings. Built as separate pages by separate efforts, those pieces would have felt like different businesses.

The decision

I designed and built the whole presence as a single system: the Figma design, the Apex Derm landing site, a chatbot reservation flow, and QDerm online booking with calendar sync and email notifications, all sharing one design language and one path for the patient.

The process

Design began in Figma, where the clinic's pages were worked out as one family rather than one-off layouts. The build followed the same structure: the landing experience, the chatbot flow for reservations, and the booking system behind it, which syncs with the calendar and confirms by email, so a booking made on the site is a booking the clinic actually holds.

The outcome

  • A complete web system delivered for one clinic: design, landing site, chatbot reservations, and online booking
  • Booking that closes the loop: calendar sync and email notification, not a form that disappears into an inbox
  • One design language across every patient touchpoint

The delivered site is live in this portfolio at /apex/. This page is the story of how it was made; the site itself is the evidence.
