At ApexDerm, we set ourselves apart through our unique data and results-driven approach to development and innovation.

Just as you wouldn’t want a sea captain with no voyaging experience to guide you across the Atlantic, the collaborative team at ApexDerm draws upon real-world, health care provider-driven experience and success to help you navigate towards and achieve your goals.

Our team of doctors consists of Canadian board-certified family physicians and dermatologists with extensive experience working in a diversity of walk-in and medical clinics, hospitals, dermatology clinics, cosmetic centres and med spas.

Our network of collaborators are also practicing dentists and pharmacists. The clinics our physicians have been a part of have included some of the most successful enterprises across Canada, with aesthetically pleasing and efficient floor plan build-outs and cosmeceutical variety driving business volume and flow to new heights.

At ApexDerm, we wanted to eliminate this financial barrier while maintaining impeccable quality of the final product. How, you may ask?

We achieve this rare feat through our hard-working CEO, who has extensive importing and exporting capabilities and access to a diverse web of international providers that are able to supply high quality materials and cosmeceuticals at only a fraction of typical North American values. Through this merger of medicine and business, we give you all the tools you need to realize your grandest aspirations in a timely and cost-effective manner.
