Eight Years at the Pass

Bartending, a famous family restaurant in Korea, a scouting at Tawara, and eight years on Sushi Umi's hot foods - the second craft, held to a professional level.

HanBin Im·15 October 2026·3 min read
UMI

My first F&B job was behind a bar at FANTACITY in 2014. My profession is IT and design - but the kitchen is the other through-line of my working life, and it runs eight years deep.

Korea: the family restaurant

In Korea I worked as a server and a manager at HWATONGSAM, a well-known restaurant - a local favourite, the kind of place with a queue - owned by my aunt. Running a floor and a family business at once teaches you service the way family teaches you anything: without anesthesia.

Tawara: where the interest took hold

Back in Vancouver I served at Tawara Sushi - and serving was where my interest in cooking itself took hold, watching the kitchen side of the pass. When Sushi Umi took over the room, its team scouted me. I asked the Tawara owner's understanding, received it, and changed sides of the counter.

Eight years on hot foods

At Sushi Umi I cooked the hot foods section steadily for eight years, to January 2026. I built a system of 21 standardized recipes for the dishes the kitchen lives on, documented so the next cook runs the station identically, and trained new cooks into it. A kitchen that depends on one person's memory is a risk; I made mine a document.

"A locals' shop that exists for real taste does not need a screen between the food and the guest."

The menu that stayed paper

Umi once asked me to prepare a QR screen menu-board. I refused it - the shop's motto is real taste for locals, and its menu should not be digital or promoted like a chain's. The digital work I did make for Umi, in 2023, was a 3D flip-book story promo instead: craft shown, ritual intact.

One more summer

In July 2026, at the owner's earnest request, I returned for three months as emergency staff - the kind of call you only make to someone whose station you trust. At home I cook fluently: any Japanese or Western dish I crave, at any difficulty. I rate the cooking professional-level. I simply hold it as a second craft - the hand's work, beside the eye's.
