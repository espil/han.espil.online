by using Crypto Currency, users can purchase digital NFT to edit, modify, and even 3D print.
The project's ultimate goal is to create the house using 3D house printers.

Season 2 Release Notes

Espil Online Shopping Center



Due to blockchain technology, all records are clear and identities are secure.
This immutable system guarantees the trust between clients and art dealers,
making it one of the most secure ways to deposit or withdraw asssets.

What the NFT lack is the reality and the freedom within the reality, and that is where Espil scratches.
Espil Online, a Virtual Mall, where users can purchase clothes online as an avatar, tries it on, and at the same day,
a drone delivers the item. CX's behaviours before, during and after the purchase can be recorded for the optimal statistic analysis.

Leo is the currency in Espil Online, it follows the user, listens to the conversation, records behaviors for the maximum security.

Leo can be grown into a full scale Espil House model.

The data is not available for stores with less than 1,000 people per day.
